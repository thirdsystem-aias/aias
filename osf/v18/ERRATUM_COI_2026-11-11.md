# Erratum: conflict-of-interest disclosure (November 11, 2026)

Paper: v0.18

The paper's declaration states that the author is employed by Samsung Electronics America and that Samsung had no role in the design, execution, analysis, or interpretation of the work. It makes no statement about the brands in the panel, and the v0.18 records hold no brand-level conflict-of-interest screen.

Disclosure: a screen of the 24 v0.18 brands, recorded in v0.63 DEVIATIONS Entry 0, item 5 (November 11, 2026; OSF v63/anchor/DEVIATIONS.md), found one tie. Ellis Brooklyn (Cell B) was launched exclusively in Korea by Samsung C&T's fashion division through LABEL C (Asia Economy, December 28, 2022: https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2022122810425090511). Samsung C&T's fashion division later withdrew from LABEL C, run from 2020 for about four years (Dealsite, March 17, 2025: https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/138084); whether the distribution continued is not established. This is a distribution relationship, not ownership. No tie was found for the other 23 brands in the sources checked, which is not a finding that they are unaffiliated. Later studies that reuse the v0.18 registry handle Ellis Brooklyn as Denon is handled: it stays in the canonical panel, and results are also reported without it (v0.63: rule R2 and sensitivity S1).

Correction: the pre-registration (§1.3 and §1.5) and the paper (§2.2) name LVMH as Byredo's owner. Puig announced on May 31, 2022 that it had acquired a majority stake in Byredo (Puig press release, May 31, 2022: https://uploads.puig.com/uploads/Puig_Byredo_May31_2022_English_05a5c902e7.pdf); Puig's 2022 annual report dates the acquisition to June 2022 (https://uploads.puig.com/uploads/Puig_2022_annual_report_ENG_f230274dfd.pdf). Only the owner's name is corrected; no result depends on it.

The paper's data, analyses and findings are unchanged. The program's later documents carry this disclosure (v0.63 pre-registration, §14, November 11, 2026).
