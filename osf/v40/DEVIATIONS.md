**What changes.**

1. **Coding-only step (step 8).** `scripts/code_only_v0_40.py` imports the locked `score_v0_40.py` and runs exactly the coding path that `score_v0_40.main()` uses: `load_registry`, `switch_controls` (Discover option (a)), `Scorer.__init__` (which codes every answer, including the §5.9 handling) and `Scorer.mention_rows`. 2. **Scripted audit draw.** `scripts/audit_draw_v0_40.py` uses seed 4040 and runs after step 8, before any verdict exists (it refuses to run otherwise). As §5.5 says, it draws 100 recommendation answers that contain at least one ghost or control mention (all of them if there are fewer), stratified by arm and condition (L|S0, C|S0, C|S1), and 100 status (S) answers, stratified by arm, condition and the role of the probe's subject (ghost, control, incumbent; from `prereg/probes_v0_40.json`).
   - *Allocation:* within each set, proportional to stratum size, by largest remainder (ties go by stratum key order).
   - *Selection:* within a stratum, `random.Random(4040).sample` over the stratum's sorted call IDs. Strata are taken in sorted key order, with one generator used first for the recommendation set and then for the status set.
   - *Frame:* the coded answers of step 8. Ambiguous mentions count toward the frame, because ambiguity is one of the audited fields. The extra Gemini 2.5 Flash pass (M5X) is part of the legacy arm's frame, since §5.5 does not exclude it. Answers that §5.9 item 1 treats as missing are not coded, so they are not in the frame.
   - *Outputs:* `audit/v40_audit_sample.json` and `.csv`, plus the manifest `audit/AUDIT_SAMPLE_v0_40.sha256` (the sample files and the inputs it was drawn from). The script is deterministic and refuses to overwrite a different sample.

It rewrites `data/v40_mentions.csv`, whose SHA-256 must equal that of the step 8 file (recorded in `data/v40_coding_manifest.json`). A difference stops the step and requires a new DEVIATIONS entry.
