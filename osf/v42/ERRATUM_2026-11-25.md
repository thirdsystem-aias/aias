# v0.42 (t₄ wave): erratum, 25 November 2026

**What was wrong.** The coder that decides whether an answer named a brand (`osf/v42/scripts/code_t4_v0_42.py`, the certified t₁ matchers) looks for registry names written with a straight apostrophe (U+0027): "Paula's Choice" (v0.20 skincare) and "L'Oréal Paris" (v0.21 cosmetics). When a model wrote the name with a typographic apostrophe (U+2019, "Paula’s Choice"), the answer was coded as not naming the brand. These are the only two registry names that contain an apostrophe. The v0.23 spirits matcher already accepts both forms ("Jack Daniel's", "Hendrick's").

**Size.** 46 of 12,096 brand-answer cells (Phase B, open questions) were coded "not named" and should have been "named". No cell changes the other way, no recognition (Phase A) code changes, and no other brand changes:

| Brand | Model | Search off | Search on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paula's Choice | GPT-6 Astra | 13 | 6 |
| Paula's Choice | Gemini 4 Argon | 11 | 7 |
| L'Oréal Paris | Gemini 4 Argon | 6 | 3 |

**Effect on the verdicts: none.** Re-coded with U+2019 and U+2018 read as an apostrophe (everything else unchanged):

|---|---|---|---|
| `H_Gen_Presence` (ρ, 95% interval) | 0.828 (0.722 to 0.890) | 0.827 (0.718 to 0.889) | CONFIRMED, unchanged |

| `H_Search_Presence` (ρ, 95% interval) | 0.909 (0.827 to 0.945) | 0.910 (0.829 to 0.945) | search-robust, unchanged |
