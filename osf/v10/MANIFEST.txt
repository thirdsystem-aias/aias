Reproducibility
---------------
Pipeline runs end-to-end on raw v0.9 responses with Python 3.14 and the
following packages: openai, python-dotenv, matplotlib, reportlab, pypdf.

Brand assets (Third System brand JSON, Akkurat Pro fonts) are not included
in this deposit. The brand-format report (papers/v10_naivephantom_stability.pdf)
cannot be regenerated without them; the SSRN paper PDF and chart PDFs can be.
