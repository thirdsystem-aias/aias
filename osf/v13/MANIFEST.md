## Methodological notes

- AI Presence data is **reused from the v0.9 Longitudinal Re-Baseline deposit** (OSF /v09/) for all five categories. v0.13 collected no new LLM responses. The construct-validity test is entirely on the Trends acquisition + scoring side.
- The matched-model subset is Claude Sonnet 4.6 + GPT-5.4-mini (response status = ok). Inter-model variation is factored out by the matched-subset constraint, not addressed.
- Pivot-rescaling is independent per category; pivots are validated for stability against the out-of-sample window prior to lock (Phase A).
- The n-floor is hard at 10 per wave (descriptive routing below); the alignment floor for full hypothesis evaluation is 12. Pivots are exempted from E1b per pre-reg §5.1.
- Partial-Spearman correlations control for two covariates (brand_age_years; tier ordinal) on rank-transformed residuals, with df correction k = 2.
- 47 of 93 brand-age entries (the v0.13-new entries for skincare and finance) carry DRAFT founding years pending source-URL verification per DEVIATIONS Entry 3 §3.7.2. Founding-year corrections will be applied via a future amendment.
