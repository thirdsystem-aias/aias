Here are several technology brands with significant cultural footprints:

**Apple** - Shaped design aesthetics, created product launch events as cultural moments, influenced how people think about personal technology

**Google** - Became a verb ("google it"), changed how people access information and expect answers instantly

**Microsoft** - Defined workplace computing for decades, made PC ownership mainstream

**Amazon** - Transformed shopping expectations and delivery speed norms

**Meta/Facebook** - Redefined social connection, influenced political discourse, made "friending" and "liking" universal concepts

**Netflix** - Changed "binge-watching" into standard viewing behavior, disrupted entertainment industry norms

**Tesla** - Shifted perceptions of electric vehicles from niche to aspirational

**Sony** - Through Walkman and PlayStation, shaped portable music and gaming culture across generations

**Nintendo** - Created gaming icons (Mario, Zelda) that transcended the medium

**TikTok** - Reshaped content creation, music discovery, and attention spans for younger generations

The biggest footprints arguably belong to Apple, Google, and Meta/Facebook, as they've most deeply influenced daily habits, language, and social norms beyond just their product categories.
