7. **Cisco** - Widely recognized for networking hardware and software, Cisco is a leading provider of solutions for cybersecurity, networking, and cloud computing.

8. **IBM** - Long respected for its enterprise solutions, IBM is noted for its contributions to cloud computing, AI, and quantum computing.

9. **Intel** - A key player in the semiconductor industry, Intel is known for its processors and technology innovations that power a wide range of electronic devices.

10. **Tesla** - While primarily an automotive brand, Tesla is frequently discussed for its advancements in electric vehicle technology and energy solutions.
