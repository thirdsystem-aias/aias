Here are several technology brands widely regarded for quality and reliability:

**Apple** - Known for tight hardware-software integration, long software support, and premium build quality. Strong in laptops, phones, and tablets, though at higher price points.

**Sony** - Consistently reliable across audio equipment, cameras, and gaming (PlayStation). Particularly respected in professional and consumer imaging.

**Samsung** - Leading in display technology and memory/storage components. Their flagship phones and SSDs are generally well-regarded, though quality varies more across their broad product range.

**Lenovo ThinkPad line** - The ThinkPad series specifically maintains a strong reputation for business laptop durability, keyboards, and serviceability. Other Lenovo lines are more mixed.

**Toyota/Lexus (vehicle tech)** - If including automotive, their infotainment and hybrid systems have strong long-term reliability records.

**Canon/Nikon** - Both maintain solid reputations in camera equipment, particularly for professional use and lens quality.

**Intel** - Generally reliable processors, though AMD has become equally competitive in recent years.

**A few caveats:** 
- Most brands have product tiers; flagship lines are typically more reliable than budget options
- Reliability can vary by product category within a brand
- "Best" depends on use case and personal priorities (repairability, value, features, etc.)
