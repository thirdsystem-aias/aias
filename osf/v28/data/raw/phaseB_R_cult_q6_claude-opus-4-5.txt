Here are several technology brands with particularly iconic or storied identities in popular culture:

**Apple** - Perhaps the most discussed, from the 1984 Super Bowl ad to Steve Jobs' keynotes, the "Think Different" ethos, and its association with creative industries.

**IBM** - "Big Blue" carries decades of computing history, from mainframes to Deep Blue defeating Kasparov, representing corporate computing's foundations.

**Sony** - The Walkman alone changed how people experienced music; the brand became synonymous with Japanese electronics innovation in the 1980s-90s.

**Nintendo** - Mario, the NES revival of gaming after the 1983 crash, and a reputation for whimsy that distinguishes it from competitors.

**Microsoft** - Windows' ubiquity, the Gates-era narrative, and the Xbox's cultural impact give it a complex but undeniably significant identity.

**Atari** - Though diminished commercially, it remains a powerful symbol of early gaming and 1970s-80s tech culture.

**Xerox** - Became a verb, and its PARC lab is legendary for innovations others commercialized.

**HP** - The garage origin story helped define Silicon Valley mythology.

**Tesla** - Rapidly developed a provocative identity around disruption and its founder's persona.

Each has shaped not just products but broader narratives about innovation, competition, and technology's role in daily life.
