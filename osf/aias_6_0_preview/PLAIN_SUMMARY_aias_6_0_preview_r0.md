# AIAS 6.0 Preview: plain summary (r0, Nov 10)

**What it is.** A first, provisional look at all six parts of AIAS on the newest AI models, built from the scores each part's own study produces in mid-November. It collects nothing, spends nothing, and locks before any of those scores exists.

**What it will claim.**
1. Up to six scores per brand, each labelled (provisional, retest pending, AI-judged, under review). A part that fails its own test is not shown.
2. Whether the parts are separate readings or copies of each other: "d of k parts are separate" (confirmed, partial, undetermined or falsified).
3. Whether Presence and Consistency stay separate on the new models (AIAS 2.0 tested only the retiring ones).
4. Which brands carry a full profile.

It will not publish a single 0–100 number or call any part validated.

**Choices for you (default in bold).**
1. Sentiment exists only on old models: **score the new answers in a small separate study (about $14–54)**, or show old-model Sentiment beside the profile, untested.
2. Borderline parts: **show as "under review"**, or leave out.
3. "Confirmed" means **every test clearly passes** (strict; "partial" becomes likely), or no test clearly fails.
4. **Profile only**, or a provisional single number (not recommended).
5. Ranking's retest: **wait until Dec 11**, else add later.
6. Sentiment counts as passed when **all five of its main tests pass**.
7. **Lock Wednesday or Thursday**; latest Saturday Nov 14, before t4 is scored.
