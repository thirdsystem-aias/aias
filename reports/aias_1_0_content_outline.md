## 1. Positioning

### What this report IS

A foundational program-milestone managerial record. The AIAS™ Presence Measurement Protocol has now been measured against five substrate families under one locked methodology version, and the five-family anchor base is the structural event that justifies a brand-format artifact distinct from the SSRN academic paper.

The report's audience is brand strategists, marketing-science practitioners, and executive readers tracking the AIAS™ program — readers who need the managerial implications of the synthesis surfaced without the formal hypothesis-matrix scaffolding the academic paper carries.

The report is a Third System™ brand-format event in the same series as the v0.16 → v0.21 phase reports, scaled to synthesis scope (~3× content volume; ~2.5× page count). It is the second deliverable of the AIAS™ 1.0 ship event; the SSRN paper at abstract 6817841 (May 2026) is the academic record, deposited two weeks before the report.

### What this report is NOT

- **Not an academic paper.** The SSRN synthesis paper carries the H1/H2 hypothesis framing, the ex-ante verdict matrices, the methodology lineage, and the full citation chain. The report does not duplicate those structures — it surfaces what they mean for brand practice in a parallel register.
- **Not a phase report.** Phase reports cover one substrate × one acquisition × ~3 findings. This report covers five substrate families × six measurement events × five managerial findings × the program's first measured moderator and phantom-layer anchors.
- **Not a claim of the full six-component AIAS™ composite.** Only the Presence component is measured; the report mirrors the SSRN paper's L7 scope discipline. The multi-year roadmap for the remaining five components (Ranking, Consistency, Coverage, Grounding, Sentiment) is signposted but not claimed.
- **Not a construct-validity claim, not a behavioral-correlate claim.** Predictive validity against consumer behavior, convergent validity across panel constructions, and discriminant validity against Mental and Physical Availability are Phase 3 future work. The report establishes the measurement-program milestone; consumer-behavior correlation is held out of scope.
- **Not a comparative-LLM-performance artifact.** The panel is held fixed at the six-slot v0.17 specification and reported in aggregate; per-LLM performance comparisons are not the report's subject.

## 2. Headline managerial findings (in order of surface)

The report surfaces five findings in the order below. Each finding is one PATTERNS entry (FINDING 01 through FINDING 05) in the content module.

### Finding 01 — Five-substrate empirical anchor base complete

AI Availability is now measured under one locked methodology against five distinct substrate families: kitchenware (v0.16 + v0.17), indie fragrance (v0.18), audiophile headphones (v0.19), skincare (v0.20), and cosmetics (v0.21). Six measurement events between March and May 2026, each pre-registered before acquisition, each evaluated against the same six-LLM reference panel held fixed since v0.17. The construct's verdicts hold across all five families — no substrate has driven the program to abandon the construct or rebuild the methodology at its foundation. Anchored, not provisional.

### Finding 02 — Phantom channel signature

Brand presence in AI-mediated retrieval is not panel-bounded. On the cosmetics measurement, six off-panel brands surfaced persistently in the panel's category responses — and the channel signature of those off-panel brands tracks the same Identity-Load gradient the panel-internal brands follow. Estée Lauder and Clinique appear exclusively in canonical-channel frames (authority pathway: prestige / heritage); Glossier appears exclusively in cultural-channel frames (discourse pathway: celebrity / DTC / cult). Three of six phantom brands channel-pure on the IL signature. The phantom layer is the structural reason a brand's AI Availability cannot be inferred from its absence on any single measurement panel.

### Finding 03 — Rare Beauty 1:17 as the textbook diagnostic case

A single cosmetics brand can be universally recognized at the Recognition layer (C_P = 6/6 across the panel) and overwhelmingly surfaced in cultural-channel Recall (R_cult = 17/18) while remaining essentially absent from canonical-channel Recall (R_cat = 1/18). Rare Beauty's 1:17 channel split is the program's clearest illustration of why a single-channel "AI mentions" metric loses the actionable diagnosis. Authority-channel position and discourse-channel position are separately measurable and separately manageable — they respond to disjoint inputs and can move asymmetrically far apart within a single brand.

### Finding 04 — Identity Load now empirically tracks AI channel asymmetry

The cosmetics measurement produced the program's first CONFIRMED moderator verdict at any layer. Channel asymmetry δ = mean(R_cult) − mean(R_cat) per cell tracks the Identity-Load gradient of the cell construction: Cell A (medium-IL prestige) δ = −3.13 with 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero in the canonical-leading direction; Cell B (high-IL celebrity-DTC) δ = +7.13 with CI excluding zero in the cultural-leading direction; Cell C (low-IL drugstore-mass) δ = +1.00 satisfying the monotonic-gradient check between the focal cells. Identity Load is operationalized through the substrate's panel design (cell construction), and that design-layer property now empirically predicts the AI channel where the brand will surface.

### Finding 05 — Three v1.6 protocol increments as managerial methodology framing

The protocol shipped three increments in v1.6 (SSRN 6816340, May 2026) that closed measurement gaps the prior versions could not address. (i) **Substrate Recognition pre-screen:** when every panel brand is categorically recognized (as on cosmetics), the prior framework returned FALSIFIED — conflating "substrate failed the test" with "substrate is too maximally-coded for the test to apply." v1.6 routes the latter case distinctly, preserving the substrate's evidentiary value at the moderator and phantom layers. (ii) **Independent moderator pathway:** the IL gradient is now evaluable independently of the prior framework's regime conditions, enabling the Finding 04 CONFIRMED verdict. (iii) **Phantom Brand Persistence as a measured component:** off-panel brand presence (Finding 02) is now scored against an exogenously-constructed reference vocabulary with a persistence threshold and validity anchor — lifting the construct from a side observation to a measured component.

## 3. P1–P5 propositional framework

Each finding maps to one proposition the report makes. The PROPOSITIONS table in the content module (the report's replacement for v21's HYPOTHESIS_SCORING) carries the five propositions with their managerial-register status. Voice register: each P reads as a proposition about what brands can do or know, not as an academic claim about what the construct is.

### P1 — AI Availability is now a measurable third channel of brand presence

AI Availability is a measurable third channel of brand presence — alongside Ehrenberg-Bass Mental Availability and Physical Availability. Five substrate families (kitchenware, fragrance, audio, skincare, cosmetics) establish the construct as cross-category, not category-specific. The construct is anchored on the data the program has shipped.

- **Status:** ANCHORED across five substrate families.
- **Evidence anchor:** Synthesis paper §4 opener; §5.1 (L1); §6.1.

### P2 — Off-panel brand presence is real, and tracks Identity Load

Brands not on a measurement panel can still drive AI retrieval. Estée Lauder and Clinique surface canonical-pure (the authority pathway: prestige / heritage); Glossier surfaces cultural-pure (the discourse pathway: celebrity / DTC / cult). Three of six phantom brands channel-pure in cosmetics. The phantom layer's channel signature is auditable — a brand's AI Availability cannot be inferred from absence on any single measurement panel.

- **Status:** CALIBRATED on cosmetics (v0.21); replication across substrates is the genuine generalization test.
- **Evidence anchor:** Synthesis paper §4.5 (phantom paragraph); §5.6 (L6); §6.2 (phantom paragraph).

### P3 — Recall splits into two channels that can be managed separately

Recall splits into a canonical channel (R_cat — authority surfaces: editorial, expert recommendation, category-best curation) and a cultural channel (R_cult — discourse density: social media, celebrity endorsement, viral content). The two operate on disjoint inputs and can be managed separately. Rare Beauty is the textbook diagnostic: C_P = 6/6 (universally recognized), R_cat = 1, R_cult = 17. The two channels can move asymmetrically far apart within a single brand — conflating them under a single "AI mentions" metric loses the actionable diagnosis.

- **Status:** CONFIRMED two-channel decomposition (Rare Beauty 1:17 textbook anchor).
- **Evidence anchor:** Synthesis paper §4.5 (Type 2 paragraph); §5.3 (L3); §6.2 (brand-practice paragraph).

### P4 — Identity Load predicts where in the two-channel space a brand will land

Identity Load predicts the AI channel where a brand will surface. Cosmetics Cell B R_cult-lead 2.83× is the strongest channel-asymmetry signal the program has produced — 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero in the IL-predicted direction. A brand's cell position (and the cell's IL construction) predicts which channel will lead.

- **Status:** CONFIRMED (program's first CONFIRMED moderator verdict at any layer).
- **Evidence anchor:** Synthesis paper §4.5 (IL Direct paragraph); §5.5 (L5); §6.2 (brand-practice paragraph).

### P5 — Methodology v1.6 closes the operational layer

Methodology v1.6 (SSRN 6816340, May 2026) shipped three increments: a substrate Recognition pre-screen for categories where every brand is categorically recognized (cosmetics); an independent moderator pathway exposing the IL signal the prior framework couldn't surface; Phantom Brand Persistence lifted from side observation to measured component. AIAS™ 1.0 = the Presence component of the AIAS construct. The full six-component composite (Ranking, Consistency, Coverage, Grounding, Sentiment) is the multi-year roadmap — not the present claim.

- **Status:** SHIPPED v1.6 (May 2026); all three increments load-bearing for the v0.21 verdicts above.
- **Evidence anchor:** Synthesis paper §3 (methodology paragraph); v1.6 methodology paper SSRN 6816340.

## 4. Content-module section structure

`reports/aias_1_0_content.py` mirrors the v21 schema with one rename (HYPOTHESIS_* → PROPOSITION_*) and proportional expansion. Section-by-section:

| Attribute | v21 baseline | Synthesis report | Volume delta |

Surgical change: ~10 builder lines, mostly column-header literals and content attribute names.

1. Cover page (cover template)
2. Lead spread: STANDFIRST + LEAD_DECK + EXEC_SUMMARY (lead template; spills to body)
3. WHAT_WE_MEASURED (body template)

### D7 — Pre-registration handling

Brand-format reports in the AIAS™ program have not historically required pre-reg — they are managerial deliverables consuming the academic record, not measurement artifacts. v0.16 → v0.21 phase reports shipped without separate pre-reg tags (each phase's `v0.NN-prereg-rN` tag covered the upstream measurement only).

**Question:** Does the synthesis-scale event warrant a deviation?

**Argument for deviation:** This is the program's first synthesis-scale brand-format event. A `aias-1-0-report-locked` tag at content-module lock would create a citeable artifact for the report's framing (the P1–P5 propositional structure, the managerial-register status vocabulary), separable from the SSRN paper's `aias-1-0-paper-locked` tag.

**Argument against:** The report consumes the academic record (SSRN 6817841 already locked at `aias-1-0-paper-locked`). The managerial framing translates the academic content but does not introduce new measurement claims, new verdicts, or new hypotheses. Pre-reg discipline applies to measurement events; a translation deliverable does not introduce a new measurement event.

**Recommended decision:** Confirm the historical convention — no separate pre-reg for the report. The SSRN paper's `aias-1-0-paper-locked` tag is the upstream lock; the report's lock event is its OSF deposit (a `git commit` reference at deposit time is sufficient for traceability). If audit pressure later warrants a citeable framing artifact, cut `aias-1-0-report-locked` retrospectively at the OSF-deposit commit.
