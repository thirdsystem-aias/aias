# Erratum (November 25, 2026)

Paper: AIAS 1.0 synthesis, "AI Availability as a Third Measurable Layer of Brand Availability" (SSRN 6817841; OSF aias_1_0/paper/aias_1_0_synthesis_paper.pdf, May 23, 2026, 27 pages, SHA-256 c3974144206e4fb384ae2ad1ff81d514ef0c1c628be43aff122ec5db49cda095)

This note revises the erratum of November 12, 2026 (aias_1_0/paper/ERRATUM_2026-11-12.md), which stays posted. Every statement of that note was checked again on November 25, 2026 against the published PDF, the phase papers v0.16 to v0.22, their OSF deposits, the methodology paper v1.6 and the program's git history. Each correction was confirmed; none was withdrawn. Four items now say more precisely where the paper's wording appears and where the correct value comes from: item 1 (the exact wording in §1), item 3 (when the taxonomy was set out), item 5 (the source of the 0.875 figure, and the same wording in Protocol v1.6) and item 9 (the record showing that v0.21 had been scored before the test was written). Page numbers refer to the published PDF.

The paper's data, scoring, numbers and verdicts are unchanged, and the published PDF is unchanged. The paper describes parts of the earlier phases (v0.16 to v0.19) wrongly, and it presents its moderator result as confirmatory when the test was specified after the data were scored. The statements below are corrected.

## A. Description of the phases

1. The paper states that the six-model panel was "held fixed across phases" (Abstract, p. 2) and "held fixed across the program" (§1, p. 3), and that "the measurement procedure is the same across all six phases" (§3, p. 6).

   Correction: the design changed across phases. v0.16 used a different panel: six model slots from four providers (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.7, GPT-5.4-mini, GPT-5.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Grok 4.1 Fast Reasoning) (v0.16 paper §4.4, SSRN 6791999). The panel named in §3 was used from v0.17 to v0.21, five of the six phases. v0.16 had no Recognition probe, v0.17 and v0.18 had one Recall channel of three questions, and v0.19 had two channels but no Identity Load contrast. Only v0.20 and v0.21 used the full procedure the paper describes.

2. The paper states that the kitchenware phases used three cells, "heritage-tier (Cell A), Japanese-cult-tier (Cell B), and mass-market-tier (Cell C)" (§4.1, p. 9).

   Correction: v0.16 measured 22 kitchen-knife brands in five tradition cells (Japanese, German, French, American, Chinese). v0.17 measured 16 premium cookware brands in three tradition cells (European, American, Japanese) (v0.16 and v0.17 papers, SSRN 6791999 and 6802261).

3. The paper states that "the v0.16 H_IdentityLoad_moderator leg returned PARTIAL, with the three-cell IL-gradient signature visible", and that the four-regime taxonomy was formalized in v1.2 "immediately after v0.16's acquisition" (§4.1, p. 10).

   Correction: v0.16's PARTIAL is its Regime 4 verdict, a test of the correlation between AI Presence and Google Trends search interest (bivariate correlation near zero, +0.022 and +0.003 at the two waves; partial correlation slightly positive, +0.101 and +0.080, rather than negative). v0.16 measured no Identity Load contrast; its Regime 4 PARTIAL later served as the first leg of the joint moderator test (v0.17 paper §2.2). The taxonomy was set out in v0.13 (SSRN 6750498) and formalized in Protocol v1.2 (SSRN 6761698), both before v0.16, which applied them (v0.16 paper §1 and §2, SSRN 6791999).

4. The paper states that Iwachu is "a Japanese kitchen-knives brand from the v0.17 panel's Cell B", and that v0.17's FALSIFIED-on-panel-inadequacy meant "the Recognition layer for the v0.17 panel was insufficiently discriminating" (§4.1, p. 10).

   Correction: Iwachu is a Japanese cast-iron cookware maker in v0.17's Japanese cell. All six models recognized it (C_P = 6/6), none named it in 18 answers to three category questions (0/18), and it was then excluded. FALSIFIED on panel inadequacy means that one brand failed the Recognition check and five were never named in the 18 Recall answers (the whole Japanese cell and two American brands), leaving 10 of 16 against a pre-registered floor of 12. The floor had been written for a Google Trends eligibility step, which the phase replaced with model-answer mentions before its Recall phase (v0.17 DEVIATIONS Entry 7). The joint v0.16/v0.17 moderator verdict was AMBIGUOUS (v0.17 paper, SSRN 6802261).

5. The paper states that v0.18's Regime 4 PARTIAL came with "a worst-cell modal share of 0.69", and that the Identity-Load moderator was "first articulated as a coherent cross-phase hypothesis in v0.18" (§4.2, p. 11; the 0.69 figure again on p. 12).

   Correction: 0.69 (0.688) is Cell C's top-2 share of Recall mentions, not a Recognition modal share (v0.18 paper §3.2, SSRN 6806558). The highest Recognition modal share in v0.18 was 0.875, in Cells A and C (7 of 8 brands at C_P = 6/6 in Cell A, 7 of 8 at 0/6 in Cell C; deposited v0.18 Recognition data, OSF v18/data/phase_a_results.json). The same "worst cell 0.69" wording appears in Table 1 of Protocol v1.6 (SSRN 6816340), from which the synthesis carried it. v0.18 used three Recall questions (niche, independent, perfumistas), found 9 Iwachu cases in two of three cells, and returned DISSOCIATION_PARTIAL. The moderator was already a joint hypothesis at v0.17 (v0.17 and v0.18 papers, SSRN 6802261 and 6806558).

6. The paper states that v0.19 used "single-channel q1–q6 frames", that v0.16–v0.19 "lack R_cult" because R_cult was "first acquired at v0.20", that v0.20 "produced the program's first two-channel acquisition", and that v0.19's Heritage cell "carries enough variance" (§3, §4.3, §4.4, §5.3, §6.3; pp. 7, 10, 13, 14, 16, 20, 23); Figure 3 (p. 12) gives no Iwachu counts for v0.17 and v0.18 ("narrative") and marks v0.19 "N/A (2-cell design)".

   Correction: v0.19 collected two channels, three category-anchored and three cultural-footprint questions, and reported three Type 1 cases and three Iwachu cases, all in the Boutique cell. Both of its cells failed the C2 adequacy condition (Recognition modal shares 0.500 and 0.875). Increment 2 does not apply to v0.19 because its two cells were built with uniform Identity Load, not because it lacks the cultural channel. Iwachu counts by phase are 1 (v0.17), 9 (v0.18) and 3 (v0.19); v0.16 measured no Recognition (v0.19 paper, SSRN 6809182).

7. The paper states that v0.20's moderator test "returned NARROWED on the five-leg joint" (§4.4, p. 14).

   Correction: at v0.20 the joint had four legs (v0.16, v0.17, v0.18, v0.20); the five-leg joint is v0.21's (v0.20 paper, SSRN 6811441).

8. Reference 2026j (p. 26) has the wrong title. The v0.19 paper is "Recognition Ceiling, Dissociation Replication, and Cultural-Channel Asymmetry on an Audiophile Headphones Substrate" (SSRN 6809182).

## B. Status of the moderator result

9. The paper states that v0.21's H_IdentityLoad_Direct CONFIRMED is "the program's first CONFIRMED moderator verdict at any layer" (Abstract, p. 2; §4.5, p. 17; §5.5, p. 20), and presents it among the results of pre-registered phases.

   Correction: H_IdentityLoad_Direct and the threshold K = 6 were defined in Protocol v1.6 after the v0.21 data had been scored. The v0.21 pre-registration was committed on May 22, 2026 at 15:24 (US Eastern); the Protocol v1.6 pre-registration tag was created the same day at 22:38, and its scorer and retrospective scoring were committed at 23:03 (commit 13f2b65). The v1.6 pre-registration itself (tag v1.6-prereg-r1, commit f10616a) cites v0.21's NARROWED moderator result and its Cell B cultural-channel lead of 2.83×, so v0.21 had been scored before the test was written. The v1.6 paper calls this scoring retrospective (SSRN 6816340). The v0.21 verdict on H_IdentityLoad_Direct is therefore retrospective and exploratory, not a confirmation. The moderator test that v0.21 pre-registered, the five-leg joint H_IdentityLoad_moderator, returned NARROWED (v0.21 paper, SSRN 6815378). The numbers (Cell B δ = +7.13, 95% bootstrap CI [+4.75, +10.25]; Cell A δ = −3.13; Cell C δ = +1.00) are unchanged. A later phase pre-registered under v1.6 (automotive, v0.22) reports CONFIRMED for the same hypothesis, scored with an interim statistic (the ratio of cultural to canonical mentions, heritage cell 2.42 against disruptor cell 0.75) and no confidence interval (SSRN 6829118).
