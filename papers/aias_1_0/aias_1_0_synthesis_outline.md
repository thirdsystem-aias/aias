## 1. Positioning

### What it IS

- The program's **foundational construct synthesis** — first paper to claim AI Availability as a measurable third layer of brand availability under a locked methodology and against a five-substrate empirical anchor base.
- A **methodology-anchored measurement paper**, not an outcome-correlation paper. Establishes the construct's measurability; does not test downstream consumer-behavior correlates.
- A **top-marketing-journal target** (target venue locked at D1).
- A **consolidation paper** — pulls v0.16–v0.21 phase evidence under v1.6 retrospective scoring boundaries documented in v1.6 §7 (locked, not extended post-hoc).
- A **two-deliverable event** if D6 = yes: SSRN academic paper + Third System™ brand-format report. The five-family consolidation is the natural managerial headline event.

### What it is NOT

- **Not a new acquisition phase.** No fresh panel, no new probes. Retrospective scoring only, within the v1.6 retrospective boundary.
- **Not a methodology paper.** v1.6 was the last methodology successor; v1.7 (if ever) is post-synthesis.
- **Not a claim of the full six-component AIAS composite.** Only Presence is operationalized. The synthesis explicitly names the full six-component composite as a multi-year roadmap and Presence as the currently-measured component.
- **Not a construct validity claim.** Construct validity is Phase 3 of the program (future work — predictive validity against behavioral outcomes, convergent validity across panel constructions, discriminant validity vs. Mental and Physical Availability). The synthesis claim is **measurability with falsifiable verdicts**, not full validation.
- **Not a consumer-behavior correlation paper.** The correlation between AI Availability and purchase / consideration / search behavior is not yet established and is explicitly held out of scope.
- **Not a comparative-LLM-performance paper.** The panel is held fixed at the six-slot v0.17 panel and reported in aggregate; per-LLM comparisons are not the synthesis's claim.

### What venue (D1 — LOCKED)

Target venue: **Journal of Advertising Research (JAR)** short-form track. Length target ~7K words (D7). Rationale in §6 D1.

## 4. The construct claim layers

The §5 spine of the paper. Each subsection states one claim layer, names the empirical anchors that support it across the five substrate families, and cross-references methodology v1.6 specifications.

### 4.1 Claim L1 — AI Availability as a third measurable layer

- **Statement:** AI Availability — the brand-level probability that an AI intermediary retrieves, recommends, or selects a brand in a category-anchored decision context — is a third measurable layer of brand availability alongside Ehrenberg-Bass Mental Availability and Physical Availability.
- **What "measurable" means here:** falsifiable verdicts under a pre-registered protocol against a fixed panel and locked substrate registry, with cross-substrate replicability.
- **Support:** all five substrate families produce coherent verdicts under v1.6; no substrate has driven the program to abandon the construct or fundamentally rebuild the methodology.
- **Boundary:** measurability is established; **construct validity is not** (Phase 3 future work). The synthesis explicitly distinguishes.

### 4.2 Claim L2 — Multi-component construct (Recognition × Recall)

- **Statement:** AI Availability decomposes into at least two empirically separable components: Recognition (C_P, category-membership canonicity) and Recall (R, retrieval into category-anchored open-ended responses).
- **Support:** v0.17 first Iwachu case; v0.18 multi-cell Iwachu; v0.19 panel-inadequacy informing Recognition layer; v0.20 differential C2; v0.21 uniform-saturation establishing the Recognition channel as exhaustible at the substrate level.
- **Methodology reference:** v1.4 Recognition × Recall decomposition.

### 4.3 Claim L3 — Two-channel Recall (R_cat × R_cult)

- **Statement:** Recall decomposes further into a canonical-authority channel (R_cat, anchored by best/recommended/quality frames) and a cultural-footprint channel (R_cult, anchored by popular/celebrity/viral/cult frames). The two channels operate on disjoint inputs and can be managed independently.
- **Support:** v0.20 first two-channel acquisition with cell-structured asymmetry; v0.21 Rare Beauty 1:17 channel split as the program's textbook decomposition demonstration.
- **Methodology reference:** v1.5 two-channel Recall decomposition.

### 4.4 Claim L4 — Three dissociation quadrants in the Recognition × Recall space

- **Statement:** Three canonical patterns occupy the Recognition × Recall plane:
  - **Iwachu** (C_P ≥ 5 ∧ R_cat ≤ 2): high Recognition, sparse canonical Recall.
  - **Type 1** (R_cat ≥ 5 ∧ R_cult ≤ 2): canonical-channel-preferred.
  - **Type 2** (R_cat ≤ 2 ∧ R_cult ≥ 5): cultural-channel-preferred.
- **Support:** Iwachu generalized across five substrate families with multi-cell distribution in three (v0.18, v0.20, v0.21). Type 1 surfaced in v0.20 and v0.21 prestige cells. Type 2 cleared EMERGED threshold in v0.21 after PARTIAL in v0.20.
- **Methodology reference:** v1.4 dissociation framework; v1.5 channel-decomposed Type 1 / Type 2.

### 4.5 Claim L5 — Identity-Load moderator (channel asymmetry tracks IL)

- **Statement:** Per-cell channel asymmetry δ = mean(R_cult) − mean(R_cat) tracks the Identity-Load gradient of the cell design. High-IL cells favor R_cult; medium-IL cells favor R_cat; low-IL cells lie between.
- **Support:** v0.21 H_IdentityLoad_Direct CONFIRMED (Cell B δ = +7.12, Cell A δ = -3.13, Cell C δ = +1.00 — monotonic). v0.20 H_IdentityLoad_Direct PARTIAL with substrate-specific architecture (skincare signal in Cell B vs. Cell C, not Cell A vs. Cell B). v0.18 IL-gradient signature visible in v1.4 single-channel data.
- **Methodology reference:** v1.6 Increment 2 (H_IdentityLoad_Direct, evaluable independently of Regime 4 C2 conditions).
- **Boundary:** The moderator is the IL gradient of the panel design, not a separately-measured construct of consumer identity. The synthesis is explicit that IL is operationalized through cell construction, not measured at the consumer layer.

### 4.6 Claim L6 — Phantom Brand Persistence as an empirical regularity

- **Statement:** Off-panel brands persistently surface in panel-anchored category retrieval. The phenomenon is empirically regular across the cosmetics substrate and is captured by R_phantom under a vocabulary construction that breaks the endogeneity hazard.
- **Framing (per kickoff convention):** treated as an **empirical regularity** in the Ehrenberg-Bass tradition (compare Double Jeopardy, duplication-of-purchase law) — a stable pattern recoverable through replication-discipline measurement — not as a coordinate mechanism deduced from a first-principles model.
- **Support:** v0.21 CONFIRMED (six off-panel brands clear K = 6; Glossier 12 pure cultural channel, Estée Lauder 13 pure canonical, channel signature tracks Identity Load). v0.20 case-study precursor.
- **Methodology reference:** v1.6 Increment 3 (R_phantom; reference vocabulary; persistence threshold K = 6; validity anchor).
- **Boundary:** v0.21 is the calibration anchor; v0.22+ prospective application is the genuine generalization test. The synthesis claims the construct is **operationalized and confirmed in the calibration case**, not yet **replicated across substrates** — the replication test is future work.

### 4.7 Claim L7 — The synthesis position: AIAS™ 1.0 = Presence, with the six-component composite as a multi-year roadmap

- **Statement:** AIAS™ 1.0 names the **Presence component** of the full multi-component AIAS construct. Presence is the operationalized component as of 2026-05 (Recognition × Recall, two-channel, three dissociation quadrants, IL moderator, Phantom layer).
- **The roadmap (named explicitly, dated openly):** the full AIAS composite is a multi-year program. Remaining components (named at the level of intent, not specification) ship under a Phase 4 successor. The synthesis does not pre-commit a count beyond "Presence is currently measured; additional components are future work."
- **Why the honest framing:** keeping the construct claim bounded to the measured surface area avoids over-claim. Reviewers can verify what's measured; the multi-year roadmap is signposted but not load-bearing for the present paper's conclusions.

## 6. Decisions — D1–D7 LOCKED (2026-05-22, `aias-1-0-outline-locked`)

### D1 — Target journal — LOCKED: **Journal of Advertising Research (JAR) short-form track**

- The Tri-System short-form track is already pointed at JAR per program memory — institutional consistency matters for the program's cumulative footprint.
- JAR's measurement-program profile is the cleanest fit for what the synthesis actually delivers: a falsifiable measurement protocol with cross-substrate replication. Top-tier reviewers at JM / IJRM / JCR will reasonably ask for the missing layers (construct validity, behavioral correlates) that the synthesis explicitly holds out of scope.

Considered and rejected: JM, JCR (both implicitly require consumer-behavior outcome correlates the program does not yet have); IJRM (viable scale-up if Pablo reverses D1, but the review-cycle cost is not worth the marginal venue tier for the present construct claim).
