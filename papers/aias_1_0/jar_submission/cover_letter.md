I am submitting "AI Availability as a Third Measurable Layer of Brand Availability: Five-Substrate Empirical Anchoring of the AIAS Presence Measurement Protocol" for consideration as a peer-reviewed research article in the *Journal of Advertising Research*.

**Contribution.** The paper introduces AI Availability — the probability that an AI intermediary retrieves or recommends a brand in a category-anchored decision context — as a third measurable layer of brand availability alongside Ehrenberg-Bass Mental Availability and Physical Availability. The AIAS Presence Measurement Protocol operationalizes this construct through a pre-registered measurement program anchored across five consumer-product substrate families (kitchenware, fragrance, headphones, skincare, cosmetics), a locked six-LLM reference panel, and a versioned methodology chain (v1.2 through v1.6). The paper reports three headline findings from the most recent phase (cosmetics, v0.21): Type 2 dissociation cleared the EMERGED threshold, the Identity-Load moderator reached its first CONFIRMED verdict, and Phantom Brand Persistence was operationalized and confirmed. AIAS 1.0 names the Presence component of the construct; the full six-component composite is a multi-year research roadmap, and construct validity is held explicitly as future work.

**Scope fit to JAR.** The paper bridges academic measurement discipline and practitioner applicability in the way JAR's mission emphasizes. On the academic side, the program operates under pre-registration discipline with locked panels, locked hypothesis matrices, and public data deposits at the Open Science Framework — six measurement events across five substrate families, each replicable against its locked protocol version. On the practitioner side, the two-channel Recall decomposition (canonical authority vs. cultural footprint) gives brand managers an auditable channel-level view of AI Availability with structural geometry: a brand's position on the two channels locates which intervention class applies, and the Identity-Load gradient predicts which channel dominates for a given substrate cell. The Management Slant section of the manuscript frames these practitioner implications directly.

**Scope boundary.** The paper claims measurability under a falsifiable protocol, not construct validity. Predictive validity against behavioral outcomes and discriminant validity against Mental and Physical Availability are Phase 3 future work, named explicitly in the manuscript. This bounded scope is deliberate — the measurement claim is established; the validation claim is not yet earned.
