### Abstract (paste verbatim — matches paper page 2)

```
Coverage Paper A (AIAS Methodology v1.12) introduced Cov*, the rate-adjusted breadth with which AI answers name a brand across 14 buying situations per category, and found it separable from Presence and Consistency. This pre-registered re-analysis of Paper A's answers, locked before Paper A's first model call, tests four further questions on the same 51 rated brands in five categories and the same three models (Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon), with no new model call. Situation sampling: over the 64 balanced ways of splitting the 14 situations into two halves, the split-half reliability of Cov* from the median correlation of the halves was 0.459 and the 10th percentile of the per-split reliabilities 0.172, short of the pre-registered 0.50 and 0.30; the set-sampling test is MARGINAL. Dropping the two situations of one cue type moved the brand order beyond the reference range for one of the seven cue types ("with what"; ρ = 0.780 against a 5th percentile of 0.809); the pre-registered rule allows at most one, so the cue-type test is CONFIRMED. Phantom brands: brands below the programme's recall floor did not differ in Cov* from the other brands (Cliff's δ = -0.062, p = 0.8309), nor after Presence was removed (δ = 0.062, p = 0.8345); both floor tests are FALSIFIED. None of the 126 automotive answers named a defunct brand, so their situation occupancy is UNDETERMINED. Identity load: 26 brands of the two-channel categories were rated and eligible, below the pre-registered floor of 45, so all three identity-load tests are UNDETERMINED. Brand profiles: a brand's lean towards a cue type did not repeat in the other situation of the same cue type (mean ρ = -0.022, p = 0.6144); the replication test is FALSIFIED, and under the pre-registered rule no typology is reported. No rated brand had Cov* above 50. Sensitivity analyses, which never change a verdict, leave both floor tests UNDETERMINED whenever they remove one of the ten rated phantom-flagged brands (the conflict-of-interest variant and four of the five leave-one-category-out variants); at a rating floor of 6 the floor-signature test reads MARGINAL, and the set-sampling test reads FALSIFIED with a mid-p version of the score and UNDETERMINED without the spirits category.
```
