### Abstract (paste verbatim — matches paper page 2)

```
Presence records whether AI answers name a brand; it does not record whether the brand is named across the buying situations of its category or only in a few. This pre-registered study specifies a Coverage instrument for the AIAS measurement programme and tests whether it is a separate component. Seventy brand-free questions, one per category entry point (14 per category, two for each of seven cue types), were asked in three runs to the newest flagship model of each of three providers (Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon) and to Claude Opus 5.5, over five categories and 112 brands; 900 of 900 calls were answered. The component score, Cov*, measures within each model how many situations a brand's mentions touch against the number expected if the brand were named without regard to the situation, given the same number of mentions; 51 brands met its rating floor. The situations changed which brands were named (instrument check passed: brand sets overlapped 0.406 more between runs of one question than between questions). Within category, Cov* correlated with Presence at ρ = 0.055 (H1 CONFIRMED), with Consistency at ρ = -0.060 (H2 CONFIRMED) and with the brand's own naming rate at ρ = 0.046 (H3 CONFIRMED); its split-half reliability was 0.646 (H4 CONFIRMED). Every gate leg was confirmed, and the pre-registered gate returned PASS: Coverage breadth is a component separable from Presence and Consistency, and the instrument passes to a second paper. The cross-category baseline reports raw and rate-adjusted Coverage per category, model and cue type on the newest models.
```
