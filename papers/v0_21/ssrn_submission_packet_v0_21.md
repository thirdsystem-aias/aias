## Step 3 — Abstract, Keywords, JEL

### Abstract (paste in full)

The AIAS™ Presence Measurement Protocol operationalizes AI Availability — the brand-level probability of retrieval, recommendation, or selection by an AI intermediary — as a measurable construct alongside Ehrenberg-Bass Mental Availability and Physical Availability. Protocol v1.5 (SSRN 6810758) closed the methodology layer for v1.4's multi-component Recognition × Recall construct by adding a C2 multi-statistic adequacy rule (distinct C_P values ≥ 3 ∧ modal share ≤ 0.625) and a two-channel Recall decomposition (R_cat for category-canonical retrieval, R_cult for cultural-footprint retrieval). v0.20 (SSRN 6811441) was the first prospective phase under v1.5 and produced PARTIAL Type 2 emergence on a skincare substrate. This paper reports v0.21, the first replication phase under v1.5 — holding the protocol fixed and testing it on a substrate selected to maximize Cell B Identity Load.

The substrate is cosmetics, sampled across three cells stratified by Identity Load on the consumer-discovery surface: Cell A (prestige, medium IL: MAC Cosmetics, NARS, Bobbi Brown, Tom Ford Beauty, Giorgio Armani Beauty, Hourglass, Chantecaille, Laura Mercier), Cell B (celebrity-DTC / cult, high IL: Rare Beauty, Fenty Beauty, Haus Labs, Pat McGrath Labs, Charlotte Tilbury, Huda Beauty, Kylie Cosmetics, Anastasia Beverly Hills), and Cell C (drugstore / mass, low IL: Maybelline, L'Oréal Paris, CoverGirl, Revlon, NYX Professional Makeup, e.l.f. Cosmetics, Wet n Wild, Milani Cosmetics). Each cell carries 8 brands; worldwide n = 24 pre-floor. The reference panel is the locked six-slot v0.17 panel. Phase B uses a six-frame battery splitting cleanly across the v1.5 channels: q1–q3 anchor R_cat (best, makeup-artist-recommended, highest quality); q4–q6 anchor R_cult (popular, celebrity, viral/cult). Four pre-registered hypotheses are tested orthogonally.

The four hypotheses returned the following verdicts. H_Type2_emergence — the primary hypothesis for v0.21 — returned EMERGED with three Cell B cases clearing the ex-ante threshold (Rare Beauty: R_cat = 1, R_cult = 17; Huda Beauty: R_cat = 0, R_cult = 11; Kylie Cosmetics: R_cat = 0, R_cult = 5), plus a fourth Type 2 case in Cell C (e.l.f. Cosmetics: R_cat = 1, R_cult = 9) — the first out-of-cell Type 2 surface in the program. Rare Beauty's 1:17 channel split is the program's textbook Recognition × Recall dissociation. H_Regime4_cosmetics returned FALSIFIED at the regime-floor: C2 fails in all three cells by identical uniform-saturation shape (distinct = 1, modal share = 1.000 per cell). Cosmetics is a maximally-coded category at the Recognition channel — every panel brand scores C_P = 6/6 — collapsing the v1.5 framework's discrimination signal entirely into Recall. H_Dissociation_substrate_generalization returned GENERALIZED with Iwachu-pattern cases (C_P ≥ 5 ∧ R_cat ≤ 2) distributed across all three cells (13 cases total, the largest single-phase count in the program); the v1.4/v1.5 multi-component construct is now anchored across five substrate families. H_IdentityLoad_moderator (five-leg joint over v0.16 × v0.17 × v0.18 × v0.20 × v0.21; v0.19 excluded for uniform-IL design) returned NARROWED, driven by the v0.21 H_Regime4 falsification, despite the cleanest substantive IL channel-asymmetry signature in the program (Cell B R_cult-lead 2.83×; Cell A R_cat-lead 2.20×).

The v0.21 acquisition also surfaces an empirical regularity in the off-panel residual: Glossier (a v0.20 Cell B panel brand) appeared in cultural-channel frames for all six panel LLMs in q6 and three of six in q4 and q5 — the strongest single Phantom Brand Persistence demonstration in the program. Estée Lauder appeared in the canonical channel for all six models (R_cat = 13/18). The panel-internal scoring is correct under the locked methodology; the off-panel pattern is identified as a Protocol v1.6 measurement candidate. For AIAS™ 1.0's foundational construct, v0.21 completes the substrate breadth requirement (five families anchored, Type 2 quadrant populated above the EMERGED threshold for the first time) and identifies three v1.6 protocol increments. Pre-registration discipline (tag v0.21-prereg-r1, deposited at osf.io/ec6wh/v21/) locks panel, hypotheses, decision rules, and verdict matrices ex-ante.
