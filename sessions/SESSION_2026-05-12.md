1. **Tea Box query-phrase ambiguity** — bare canonical `Tea Box` query captures generic gift-box/subscription phrasing far beyond the actual brand. Rescaled mean is 166-169 worldwide (~1.67× Twinings) and 406-428 US (~4× Twinings). Implausible for a small Indian D2C retailer. Plan: keep Tea Box in primary analysis per pre-reg, but run a Tea Box-excluded sensitivity analysis at scoring stage; document in limitations.

2. **Bundle 3 pivot suppression** — Twinings mean was 30.2 worldwide / 8.9 US in Bundle 3 vs ~85 in other bundles. Driven by Tea Box (worldwide) and likely Harney & Sons or a combination (US). This is mechanically correct bundle behavior; rescaling math handles it.

3. **Whittard of Chelsea US t1 collapse** — mean=0, sparse flag. Effective n_eligible reduces by 1 in US t1 only. British heritage brand with thin US Trends presence.

4. **Twinings E1b=N is a pivot artifact** — rescales to exactly 100.0 every day, sd=0. v0.13 had the same artifact for CeraVe/YNAB. Pivot is included in scoring regardless of E1b flag.

5. **Chinese cell alternate activation** — only Yunnan Sourcing passed (mean=28.43). Wang De Chuan and In Pursuit of Tea failed both solo and E5 bundled. Chinese cell lands at 3 eligible (target 4 not reached, but at pre-reg per-cell floor of 3).
