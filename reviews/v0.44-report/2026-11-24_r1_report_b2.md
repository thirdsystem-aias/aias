## Brief 2 (Verdict and framing): TS-FL-v0.44-r1, round r1, 2026-11-24

**What I checked.** I read pre-registration §§1–2.6 (H_Spread, H_Tier and H_Separation, their verdict rules, the manipulation checks and descriptives 1–9), `v44_verdicts.json`, DEVIATIONS Entries 0–3 and the paper's Discussion, Limitations and Declarations. I compared them with the report's cover, At a glance, Introduction, the five finding headlines, chart titles, insight lines and captions, the tests table, Limitations and Disclosures. I looked at renders p-07 and p-08.

**What holds.**
- **Verdicts:** all three match `verdicts.*.verdict`, which is CONFIRMED for each:
  - H_Spread: 8 of 8 industries show spread, against a bar of 6.
  - H_Tier: ρ 0.558, p 9.999e-05, 95% interval 0.408–0.677.
  - H_Separation: ρ 0.019, interval −0.249 to 0.269, 85 rated brands.
  - Nothing is reversed, and no null or UNDETERMINED result is presented as positive.
- **Findings 04 and 05:** both are labelled DESCRIPTIVE, with "A pre-set description, with no test". They match descriptives 2, 4 and 5.
- **Omnibus comparison:** the "5 of 5" comparison is called a comparison made after scoring, with no verdict, as in paper §3.3 and limitation 10.
- **Deviations:** DEVIATIONS Entries 1, 2 and 3 are each reported. The two refused starts are stated, and nothing was sent in either.
- **Quality checks:** all the manipulation checks reported in the verdict file passed. MC_Alias appears as "exported, not reviewed".
- **Limits:** the main limits travel with their claims:
  - Finding 03 says rating leaves out most smaller brands and "says nothing about Consistency among rarely named brands".
  - Finding 05 says the data cover one night and that search was off.
  - Finding 04 says whether answers presented the departed brands as current was not coded.
- **Things not present:** no H_ labels, no mention of v0.38 or v0.39, and no causal wording. The one "lowers their measured naming" (hotel groups) is the paper's own limitation 3.
- **Minus sign:** the "0.249" without a minus in the Finding 03 insight line is a text-extraction artifact. The render (p-08) shows "−0.249".
