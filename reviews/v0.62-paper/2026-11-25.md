## Brief 4: editor screen (no journal named)

As an SSRN or OSF study paper it is fit to post after the fixes. For a journal, the three hardest reviewer objections, starting from the outside-review themes, are:
1. **Translation validity.** Model-made translations with no native review; low inter-model κ (Table 6). The paper answers with limitations 1–2 and S-Trans. A reviewer will still want a native audit of at least a sample.
2. **Measurement reliability and nondeterminism** (outside review: "AIAS score risks being a snapshot"). The paper answers with three runs, rel1/rel3 (Table A5) and limitation 5 (time and language confounded, t5 pending). Weak Phi agreement in KO/ZH feeds this objection.
3. **Construct scope.** Language without a market, US registries, and "measurement invariance" in the keywords (F8). The paper answers with limitations 3–4; adding the cross-lingual LLM literature (F8) would pre-empt it.

Recommendation: send after fixes F1–F9 and P1. Not a desk reject on content; journal fit is unknown.
