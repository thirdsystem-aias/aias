Other checks:
- **Causal wording.** Apart from B2-6, B2-8 and B2-10, I found none. §4 states "an association, not its cause".
- **"Recommended" versus "named".** The paper uses "named" throughout. "Recommendation" appears only as the name of the question type.
- **Missing tests.** All six tests and all four manipulation checks are reported. S-Legal, S-Operating, S-SunCountry, S-D4, S-Discover, S-Arm and S-Length are all reported.
- **Coder-reliability marking.** The overall threshold was met, so the pre-registered "coder reliability below threshold" marking does not apply. The paper still reports the subset κ of 0.487, which goes beyond what the rule requires and is good practice.

**Verdict: blocked.** B2-1 is a pre-registered reporting commitment (per-ghost and per-model results) that is neither met nor declared in DEVIATIONS; B2-15 needs Pablo.
